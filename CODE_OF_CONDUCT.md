# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback on technical grounds.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers at the contact address listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (the "private report" channel). Maintainers will
review reports and take proportionate action, up to removing a participant from
the project. Reports are handled confidentially.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).
